The founder members of PCS have mutually decided and agreed to establish, as on and from the 1st day of August, Two Thousand and Twenty-Six (2026), an association to be registered under the relevant Cooperative Societies Act of Bangladesh, to be known as Progressive Cooperative Society, for the mutual socio-economic benefit, welfare, and advancement of its members.
Goals, Mission, and Vision
The vision outlines the ultimate long-term objectives and aspirations of the Association:
To establish a thriving, affordable, and sustainable housing community where every member is guaranteed access to a safe, modern, and high-quality residence.
To emerge as the premier cooperative model for ethical, transparent, and eco-friendly urban construction and real estate development.
Members' Interest and Shareholding Structure
The total preliminary interest and capital of the members in the fund shall be divided into Thirty (30) equal shares. A member may hold more than one share, either individually or jointly. However, in accordance with the cooperative laws of Bangladesh, no single member shall hold more than one-fifth (20%) of the total shares. The shareholding structure shall be reviewed, amended, or updated from time to time by the Executive Committee, subject to the final approval of the General Body in the Annual General Meeting (AGM).

Major Terms and Conditions
Admission Fee: The Admission Fee for enrolling in the Association is fixed at BDT 5,000 (Bangladeshi Taka Five Thousand only), which is non-refundable under any circumstances or upon cessation of membership.
Monthly Subscription and Payment Schedule: Every registered member shall deposit the prescribed monthly subscription amount X write amount XX into the bank account or directly to the Finance and Accounts Division within the 12th day of each calendar month.
Lumpsum Special Payments: Every member shall deposit a lumpsum amount of BDT 10,000 (Bangladeshi Taka Ten Thousand only) after 6 months but not later than 9 months from the date of incorporation/registration. This lumpsum collection process shall continue and repeat every six (6) months of the Association's operation. Furthermore, all members are legally bound to pay any additional amount determined by the EC at the time of acquiring any piece of land or project execution, proportionate to their shareholding.
Penalty for Late Payment and Default: The due date for monthly payments is the 12th of each month. If a member fails to deposit the subscription by the due date, he/she must clear the dues on or before the 18th day of the respective month along with a fixed late penalty of BDT 150 (One Hundred and Fifty only). Failure to pay within the 18th day shall attract an additional penalty of 15% on the outstanding amount plus the basic penalty of BDT 150. If a member fails to clear three (3) consecutive monthly installments, his/her membership shall be liable to automatic cancellation, and the Membership Cancellation Policy shall apply.
Membership Cancellation and Settlement Policy: If any member voluntarily applies for the cancellation or withdrawal of their membership, the initial Admission Fee shall be forfeited, and an additional 30% of the total accumulated deposited money shall be forfeited by the Association towards administrative and operational expenses. The remaining 70% of the deposited funds shall be refunded/distributed to the outgoing member in the subsequent quarter after the Accounts Division has completed all legal, audit, and structural formalities.
Bankers and Financial Operation
The Trustees/Executive Committee shall select a scheduled commercial bank in Bangladesh to act as the official Bankers of the Fund. All subscription amounts and institutional funds must be kept in the bank account. Sums may be withdrawn from the banking account at any time under the joint signatures of any Two (2) out of the Five (5) authorized Executive Committee members for making official payments and settlements.
Accounts, Transparency, and Audit
Each member shall be provided with an official digital acknowledgment or receipt immediately upon payment by the Accounts and Finance Division through the official WhatsApp group or registered email. The Accounts and Finance Division shall prepare the comprehensive annual accounts, financial statements, and audit reports of the Association at the end of each fiscal year and present them to all general members during the Annual General Meeting (AGM) once a year.
Liquidation and Dissolution of Fund
Subject to the provisions of the Cooperative Societies Act, the Association may be dissolved or liquidated only by a unanimous decision of the Executive Committee taken in a special meeting specifically convened for this purpose, and subject to the written approval of the Advisory Panel. In such an event, the assets and properties of the Association, if any, remaining after fully satisfying all valid claims, liabilities, debts to members, or any third party, shall be distributed among the existing valid members in proportion to their shareholding interest.
